About Our Mission

Crochet for Warmth was founded with a simple goal: bring people together through craft while making a real difference in our local community.

Winter can be tough, but a hand-crocheted scarf or blanket is more than just insulation—it's a tangible reminder that someone cares. We partner with local charities, homeless shelters, and children's hospitals to deliver warm items directly to those who need them most.

No experience? No problem! We teach beginners, share yarn supplies, and enjoy good conversation while crafting together.
