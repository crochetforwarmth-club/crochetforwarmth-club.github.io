CROCHET FOR WARMTH

Crafting Comfort, Sharing Love

A youth-led organization dedicated to crocheting warm clothing for those in need, raising awareness, and spreading warmth in our community.

What We Do

Our mission extends beyond yarn and hooks. Here is how we are helping our community stay warm and connected.

🧶 Handmade Warmth

Crocheting handmade warm clothing for people in need during the harsh winter months.

📦 Donation Drives

Hosting and organizing clothing and yarn donation drives to support our projects.

🤝 Community Initiatives

Running fundraising events and engaging in community outreach initiatives.

🏠 Shelter Partnerships

Working directly with local shelters and community organizations to distribute items.

🌟 Youth Leadership

Fostering youth leadership and providing meaningful volunteering opportunities for students.

❤️ Raising Awareness

Educating the public and working to help reduce the stigma surrounding homelessness.
