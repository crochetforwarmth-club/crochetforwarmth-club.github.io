Crochet for Warmth

Homelessness doesn't always look like homelessness.

We're a youth-led nonprofit turning yarn and volunteer hours into hats, scarves, blankets, and mittens for people experiencing homelessness within our community. Every stitch is made by someone who wanted to help. Every item goes to someone who needs it.

What we do

Crochet for Warmth brings people together to make sure fewer people spend winter cold.

🧶

Handmade clothing

Volunteers crochet hats, scarves, blankets, mittens, and socks that go directly to people experiencing homelessness in our community.

📦

Donation drives

We organize clothing and yarn drives so gently-used warm items and crafting supplies reach the people and the volunteers who actually need them.

💛

Fundraising & community initiatives

From school sales to online campaigns, our fundraisers cover yarn, tools, and outreach costs so every dollar goes further.

🏠

Shelters & community partners

We work directly with local shelters and organizations to get finished items to where they're needed most.

🌟

Youth leadership

Students lead chapters, run events, and manage donations. This builds real leadership experience while giving back to the community.

📣

Reducing stigma

We share stories and facts about homelessness to build understanding and chip away at the stigma about homelessness.

Start or join a chapter

Bring Crochet for Warmth to your school. A chapter is a student-run group that crochets, fundraises, and spreads the word in your own community.

  1. Request our starter package

    We'll send you our chapter guidelines, forms, and everything you need to get going.

  2. Stay connected

    Reach out! We'll help with resources, hour verification, and connecting you with other chapters.

  3. Recruit a few members

    Find a few classmates and a teacher or staff sponsor to support your chapter.

  4. Host your first meeting

    Teach the basics, pick a first project, and start crocheting together.

Ready to start a chapter?

Download our starter package to see everything a chapter needs, or reach out and we'll walk you through it.
